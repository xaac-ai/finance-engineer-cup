Finance Engineer Cup Challenge Instructions
Build a reusable workflow to check RelayCloud customer contracts and usage against billing,
explain findings and recommend actions. All companies, people and records are fictional.
Use any tools or interface; no live system access is needed.
REQUIRED SCOPE: October 1-31, 2026 service and its effect on October books. Close cutoff:
2026-11-11T18:00:00-07:00. This is business day 8 using Monday-Friday and ignoring public
holidays. The calendar definition only establishes the cutoff; no business-day calculation
is required.
Use adjacent-period records where needed to explain October. Flag later-service issues as
follow-ups; do not reconcile November or rebuild future contract history. Compare expected
and actual customer billing with platform fees and usage shown separately. An annual
prepaid platform fee is not billed again in October, but October earned revenue still
exists.
For advance subscriptions, reconcile the invoice billing periods covering October and
identify any additional October-service charges. You may instead show explicit day-based
allocations to October, provided your basis is clear. Neither a full crossing-period
invoice nor its billing allocation automatically equals October recognized revenue. Both
well-supported presentation approaches are accepted.
Show errors, valid explanations and uncertainty with customer, affected period, supportable
amounts, evidence and recommended next step. Customer complaints are not required to
identify a finding. Results can be inside your build or exported; a separately formatted
exception register is not required.
Submit the working build with run instructions, findings and supported October journal
entries. A 3-5 minute walkthrough should demonstrate one important finding, one judgment
call and a rerun; the artifact carries remaining detail. AI is welcome, but extracted terms
and calculations must be reviewable. No future revenue schedule or system-record export is
required; recommend updates instead.
COMPANY RULES: One USD entity/book, one platform subscription and one workflow-run usage
SKU. Tax, FX, collections, cash settlement, processing fees, marketplace sales, breakage
and complex multi-obligation arrangements are excluded.
PAYMENT AND DATE DISPLAY: Order forms use Net 30 payment terms. Collections, aging and
settlement remain outside the exercise; unpaid invoices alone do not imply a required
adjustment. Visible service ends are inclusive; the equivalent end-exclusive boundary is
stated in the commercial terms and retained in system exports.

SOURCE PRECEDENCE: Executed order forms and amendments determine commercial terms. An
amendment changes only named terms; unchanged terms persist. Register
supersedes_document_id denotes partial precedence, not cancellation of the original order.
START_HERE defines exercise accounting policy; messages establish approvals and context.
USAGE: Aggregate unique active records across a customer’s verified product accounts before
applying its calendar-month allowance. No rollover or tiers. Daily records are additive
across product accounts but versions/duplicates must not be double counted. Explicit zero-
use days remain records. record_status and replaces_record_id describe source corrections;
there are no superseded versions in this release.
Partial first-month allowance: monthly allowance x active calendar days / days in month,
rounded once to whole runs half up. For an allowance amendment, weight each allowance by
applicable calendar days and round once. Charges equal max(0, eligible units minus
allowance) x signed overage rate, rounded once to USD cents half up. Prices are negotiated
net prices, with no additional invoice discounts.
UNVERIFIED USAGE: Reconcile known mapped activity separately and isolate unverified usage.
Do not force a mapping, assume the remaining customer owns a workspace, or treat missing
attribution as zero usage. Document conditional exposure and request Product Operations
evidence. An unapproved concession request alone does not authorize a credit or revenue
reduction.
BILLING: Platform invoices are issued in advance on contract billing anchors, either
monthly or annually. Usage is always calendar-month based and separately invoiced on the
first day of the next month. An absent usage invoice can be valid when no overage exists.
Include invoices issued by cutoff; November 15 invoices have not yet been issued. September
platform invoices are settled; October/November invoices remain unpaid.
ACCOUNTING: Recognize platform consideration by actual service days / 365, independent of
invoice frequency. Preserve the original base schedule when adding an upgrade increment.
Round each monthly recognition component once to cents, with final-period rounding
allocated to the last period. The signed amendment defines incremental consideration and
billing conventions.
Platform invoice: Dr 1100 AR / Cr 2200 deferred platform revenue. Recognition: Dr 2200 / Cr
4000 platform revenue. Any customer platform net debit in 2200 is reclassified Dr 1160
platform contract asset / Cr 2200. Reverse any prior reclass before recalculating. The
supplied October GL ALREADY contains its baseline reclass. Propose only incremental changes
after correcting recognition. Do not net different customers or platform assets against
usage.

FORMATS: UTF-8 comma-delimited CSV, one header, blank means missing/not applicable rather
